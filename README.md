# AniLog

I made AniLog to keep my anime and visual novels in one place. It's a spot to track what I'm watching or reading, see how much time I've spent on a series, and share my favorites with friends. Light novels, games and  other additions may come in future updates.

**[Visit anilog.app](https://anilog.app).** Browse as a guest or sign in to save your library and customize your profile.
